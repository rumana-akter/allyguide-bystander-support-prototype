# Risk register

Scope: the AllyGuide demonstrator as published in this repository. Likelihood and impact are the developer's qualitative judgements (Low / Medium / High) for a public research demonstrator with live mode off by default. They have not been reviewed by specialists.

"Tests" refers to automated tests that exercise the mitigation. A mitigation without a test is marked "none (design only)".

| ID | Risk | Who could be harmed | Likelihood | Impact | Mitigation in this demonstrator | Tests | Residual risk |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| R01 | A suggestion uses inflammatory or escalatory wording | Targeted person, bystander | Medium (live mode), Low (demo) | High | Rules ESC-01 to ESC-05 withhold the option; calm, behaviour-focused templates; relative risk shown on every option | `safetyRules.test.ts` (escalation), `demoGenerator.test.ts` (no findings), fixtures F02 to F04 | Sarcasm and implied hostility are missed (fixture F13) |
| R02 | A suggestion discloses or tells the user to find personal information | Targeted person, any named party | Low | High | Rules PII-01 to PII-06 run on every text field; scenarios contain no real details; withheld text and evidence are never sent to the browser | `safetyRules.test.ts` (personal information), `liveAndSuggest.test.ts` (withheld text not sent) | Indirect identifiers (for example, a described workplace) are not detected |
| R03 | Victim-blaming or minimising language | Targeted person | Medium (live mode) | High | Rules VB-01 to VB-05; static and demo text written to avoid blame | `safetyRules.test.ts` (victim blaming), `staticGuidance.test.ts` | Implied blame is missed (F14); negated phrases are over-flagged (F16) |
| R04 | Users over-trust suggestions, or the tool overstates certainty | Bystander, targeted person | Medium | Medium | Uncertainty level and notes on every result; "checks do not mean safe" on every option; rules UC-01, UC-02 and MU-01 to MU-03 | `safetyRules.test.ts`, `demoGenerator.test.ts` (never low uncertainty), `scenario.spec.ts` (disclaimers) | Disclaimers may be ignored; real over-reliance is not measured |
| R05 | Unsafe instructions (offline confrontation, tracking people down, contacting employers, fake accounts, amplifying abuse) | Everyone involved | Low | High | Rules UI-01 to UI-05; live system prompt forbids these tactics | `safetyRules.test.ts` (unsafe instructions), fixture F09 | Paraphrased instructions can evade the patterns |
| R06 | The tool acts without the user's decision | Targeted person, bystander | Low | High | No posting or sending capability exists; `requiresUserConfirmation` checked by BU-01; claims of having acted blocked by BU-02 | `safetyRules.test.ts` (bypassing choice), `schema.test.ts` (extra fields such as auto-post rejected) | None identified for this version |
| R07 | Guidance shifts responsibility onto the person targeted | Targeted person | Low | High | Guidance addresses bystanders only; agency criterion R1 on the dashboard; VB-04 | `harness.test.ts`, `dashboard.spec.ts` | Subtle framing is not detectable by rules |
| R08 | Users submit real harassment content, creating privacy and distress risks | Users, third parties | Low | High | No free-text input anywhere; API accepts only a scenario id and provider and rejects extra fields | `liveAndSuggest.test.ts` (API route), `api.spec.ts` (extra fields rejected) | None identified for this version |
| R09 | An API key or other credential is exposed | Repository owner | Low | High | Key read only in server-only modules; `.env*` ignored by git; secret scan in CI; end-to-end check of browser bundles | `npm run check:secrets`, `liveAndSuggest.test.ts` (config), `api.spec.ts` (no key in bundles) | Depends on the operator not committing keys elsewhere |
| R10 | Prompt injection through scenario content (live mode) | Users | Low | Medium | Only fixed, developer-written fictional scenarios are sent; content wrapped and marked as data; output schema-validated and safety-checked | `liveAndSuggest.test.ts` (prompt structure, malformed output rejected) | Not tested against a real model |
| R11 | Live model is unavailable, refuses, or returns malformed output | Users | Medium (live mode) | Low | Typed error mapping; schema validation; clear error states; static guidance always available | `liveAndSuggest.test.ts` (error mapping), `scenario.spec.ts` (server and network errors) | Live mode was not exercised against the real API |
| R12 | Viewing the scenarios causes distress | Users | Medium | Medium | Content note on the home page and on every scenario; mild or paraphrased abuse; no graphic or sexual content | `scenarios.test.ts` (content notes), `home.spec.ts` | Individual reactions vary; no specialist review yet |
| R13 | The demonstrator is mistaken for a validated intervention or evidence of effectiveness | Readers, supervisors, the public | Medium | Medium | Banner on every page; statements on home, About, dashboard and README; dashboard numbers labelled synthetic | `home.spec.ts`, `dashboard.spec.ts` | Screenshots can be shared without context |
| R14 | Scenarios, rules and labels reflect one developer's perspective (English-only, limited intersectional coverage) | People whose experiences are under-represented | High | Medium | Limitations stated; one intersectional scenario; labels marked as unvalidated | none (design only) | Substantial: addressed only through co-design and expert review in the proposed PhD |
| R15 | False positives withhold helpful suggestions | Bystander | Medium | Low | Withheld options show the rule and reason; known over-flags documented (F15, F16) | `harness.test.ts` | Remains; keyword rules cannot read context |
| R16 | Vulnerable or tampered dependencies | Operators | Low | Medium | Exact version pins; versions at least two weeks old at the time of pinning; lockfile committed; `postcss` overridden to a patched version; `npm audit --audit-level=high` in CI | CI audit step (0 vulnerabilities on 2026-11-09) | New advisories appear over time; transitive versions follow the lockfile |
| R17 | Accessibility barriers | Disabled users | Medium | Medium | Semantic HTML, keyboard-operable tabs, skip link, focus styles, light and dark themes | `accessibility.spec.ts` (axe, light and dark), `responsive.spec.ts`, keyboard tab test | Automated checks find only part of the barriers; no testing with assistive-technology users |
